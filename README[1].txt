Cloudflare upload:
1. Extract this ZIP on your computer.
2. In Cloudflare 'Upload and deploy', drag the entire extracted folder OR select all four files.
3. Deploy.
4. After the temporary workers.dev/pages.dev address works, connect kjcontractinggroup.com.au.
